Fair questions,
straight answers.

Can the developer read my bookmarks?

No. There is no backend, no API, nothing calling home. Data exists only inside your browser, encrypted. The code that does it is public.

What does Sync actually send?

Only ciphertext: salt, verifier, IV, and encrypted chunks. Chrome/Brave Sync streams it between your devices — nobody along the way sees a single bookmark.

I forgot my password. Now what?

The vault is unrecoverable — permanently. By design: anything that could recover it could also be stolen. Reinstall to start fresh.

Does it slow my browser down?

Key-stretching runs once at unlock. Browsing, saving, and searching are instant. The service worker is dormant between uses.
